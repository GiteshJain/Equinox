## 1. Scans that stop part-way
Today a scan the capture starts but never finishes (the phone closed, the tab went away) stays unfinished for good.
`scan_open` only looks back an hour, so nothing reuses it, and nothing says it stopped.
- **New migration** (`CREATE OR REPLACE` and data only):
  - when a scan starts (`private.scan_ingest()`, kind `start`), every unfinished scan of the same world started more
    than an hour earlier is marked **`stopped`**, with a note giving how many airlines and airports it read;
  - the same for any unfinished scans on live when the migration runs;
  - check that every reader of `game_scans` still uses only finished scans (`status = 'done'`: the report's export,
    `competition()`, the fare ladder's rival pause, `capture_airlines()`'s `scan_due`, the briefing). List them in the
    pull request.
- **Systems:** the world scan line (`screens/systems/systems.ts`, `scanLine`) adds "the scan of <day> stopped after N
  of M airlines" when the latest scan stopped and no finished scan came after it. Amber, not red: the next capture
  scans again. `system_status()` gives what it needs.
- **Test** (`database/tests/capture_scan_test.sql`): a scan left unfinished, a new one started two hours later, the
  first one `stopped` with its note; one started within the hour is still reused as now.

## 3. The cabin charge check
When an aircraft is filed, the planner fits its plan card's cabin and logs the first fit in `spending_log` (kind
`cabin`, note "<reg> first cabin fit (<config>)") at the config's price. The game charges it on the bank statement as
"Installation of cabin interior (<reg>)" (`game_payments`, kind `cabin`; the same kind also holds "Pilot training",
which isn't a cabin). On live on 21 Oct, every first fit logged since 18 Oct matches the game's line to the dollar;
the earlier mismatches are from the refits before 18 Oct and are left alone.
- **The registration on cabin lines:** `game_payments.registration` and `aircraft_id` are empty on these lines. Fill
  them from the description for "Installation of cabin interior (<reg>)" (`private.payment_registration()` or wherever
  the capture names a line's aircraft), going forward and for the lines already there.
- **The figure:** `private.fig_cabin_charges(p_world_id, p_as_of)` and the staff function
  `public.cabin_charges(p_as_of timestamptz default now())`, read with GET, for every aircraft whose first fit was
  logged on or after the new setting `cabin_charge_check_from` (`2026-10-18`, described: "the first day the plan card
  chose the cabin; earlier fits include refits"). For each, one of:
  - **matches:** one installation line for the registration, at the logged amount (counted, not listed);
  - **differs:** the line's amount isn't the logged one: both amounts and the difference;
  - **charged twice:** more than one installation line for the registration on or after the logged day;
  - **not charged yet:** none, and the fit was logged more than 2 days before the latest captured statement line;
  - **not logged:** an installation line on or after the setting's day for a registration with no first fit logged.
  Each with the airline, the registration, the aircraft's id, the logged row's id, date, amount and config, and the
  game's line(s).
- **Today:** one Needs attention item when anything isn't a match ("2 cabin charges don't match what we logged"),
  linking to Systems. Dismissable like the others.
- **Systems:** a **Cabin charges** board: the counts, and each problem row with **Use the game's amount** on a
  differing row (updates the `spending_log` row's amount through `planner_save()`, logged as usual).
- **Test** (`database/tests/cabin_charges_test.sql`): one of each state, the setting's day respected, and the pilot
  training lines ignored.
