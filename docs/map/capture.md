# Capture

One part of the map, `docs/architecture.md`, in full. The map has the short version; a build that changes this part adds its dated note here, not in the map.

## What it is

A userscript in Gitesh's browser. It asks the database which airlines to capture (`capture_airlines()`, the capture key's world), then one button captures each airline's events, bank statement (the game's last 24 hours, read to its last page and sent in parts of at most 500 lines cut between minutes, since 1.16), income statement, balance sheet and loads, sends them to `capture_ingest()` by the airline's game ID. Each page is checked as the database stores it (#21): ok, short or broken, kept on the raw page; after the loads page (each airline's last) the reply's rows are the panel's words for the airline ("all good", or what came in short or broken), so the userscript prints the verdict without a change.

**The world scan** (since 21 Oct, version 1.5; spec `docs/specs/2026-10-21-scan-in-capture.md`): `capture_airlines()` also says whether a scan is due (the latest finished scan at least `settings.scan_every_days` capture days old, 1 to start: every evening), an unfinished one started within the hour, and the airports to read (our bases, `gscan.py`'s big rival hubs and the latest scan's 20 busiest airports). When one is due and no capture page failed, the userscript then reads every airline in the world (the 26 letter pages, each airline's schedule and, since 23 Oct (version 1.8, spec `docs/specs/2026-10-23-rival-fleets.md` §2), its overview page with its fleet by type and aircraft on order, every airline the letter pages list, flying or not) and those airport pages, exactly as `gscan.py` reads them and at its pace (0.4, 0.5 and 0.25 seconds between pages, 0.5 before each overview page), and sends them with the capture key to `capture_scan()` (start, airline, fleet, airport, finish), which stores them as `ingest_snapshot()` does, in the key's world. A failed page is logged and skipped; with more than 10% of the airlines failed the scan isn't finished (a failed overview page never counts towards that; the finish note and "World scan done" add "N fleet pages, M failed"). Its state is in the tab's `sessionStorage`, so a reload carries on; the panel shows the progress and a Stop button.

**The fleet pages** (since 22 Oct, version 1.6; spec `docs/specs/2026-10-22-fleet-capture.md`): after each airline's other pages, the userscript reads its fleet list (`/app/fleets`, which is also the Default fleet's page) and every fleet with aircraft in it (`/app/fleets/<id>`, one per hub), half a second apart, and sends each to `capture_ingest()` as page kind fleet (the run, which page of how many, the fleet, the airline's fleets and every aircraft as the page shows it; a page that doesn't read is sent with its error). The panel says "Pan Am: fleet 2 of 3".

**The panel** (since 22 Oct, version 1.7) never grows past the window: its lines scroll inside it and follow the newest one unless Gitesh scrolls up, so "Done" and "World scan done" stay in view. Fleet pages take no part in the day's completeness or the snapshot, and don't hold back the world scan.

**The market pages** (since 30 Oct, version 1.9; spec `docs/specs/2026-10-30-market-pages.md`): `capture_airlines()` also says whether they're due (the latest finished market run at least `settings.market_every_days` capture days old, 7: once a week; 0 off), an open run started within the hour, and the pages to read: for each of our active airlines, both directions of each pair it flies (routes with rotations, not removed), a pair two of ours fly listed once, the open run's pages read ok left out (since 31 Oct a page that failed is listed again, so carrying a run on reads its failures again). After the world scan (or the last airline when no scan is due), when no capture page failed and Stop wasn't pressed, the userscript switches to each airline as the fares userscript does (`/app/enterprise/dashboard?select=<game id>`), checks the first page shows it open (the page's `fixedEnterpriseId`), and reads `/app/com/markets/<FROM><TO>` by `fetch`, half a second apart: the listing's filters, Current Availability (each flight's classes, prices, seats left and status), Market Shares, our service profile on the pair (the settings form's On-Board Service, read only) and the historic weeks. When a listing filter differs from the saved setup (every class and status ticked, Ignore own flights off, Group by flight on) it sets it through that filter's own Wicket listing call, reads the page again, and stops only if one still differs, naming it; those four calls are the only posts it makes to the game, and it never sends the page's Pricing or settings forms. Since 1.10 (the first live run, 30 Oct: four airlines' pages all failed, because with Group by flight off the game shows a flat list without the grouped table and 1.9 judged the page before setting the filter) each page goes: the airline open (the first page after a switch, and any page without its table: the wrong one stops the run), the filters (set when they differ), then the page read; a page that still doesn't read is sent failed with what the game served (title, heading, address, the airline open, Group by flight). A pair where only we fly is a normal page. The service code sent is the profile's leading token ("S2 (min 800km)" → S2; `capture_market()` keeps only the token too, `private.market_service_token()`, and the service check compares by it, so the reads of 30 Oct check right). Since 1.11 (4 Nov, spec `docs/specs/2026-11-04-fare-default-from-market.md` §1) it also reads each page's Pricing panel, read only: each class's Current Price and Default Price, sent as `pricing` (a page without the panel sends none). It sends only the figures (fares by flight prefix and class, shares, weeks, pricing; never the HTML) to `capture_market()` with the capture key (start, page, finish); a page that doesn't parse is sent as failed and skipped; the airline not open or the session lost stops the run; with more than 10% failed it isn't finished. The panel says "Market pages: 120 of 686 (Pan Am)…", then "Market pages done: 686, N failed", with Stop; a reload (each switch is one) carries on from `sessionStorage`, and the run ends back on the airline the capture started with. The market pages don't touch salaries.

**Salaries** (since 5 Nov, version 1.12; since 1.15, 9 Nov, by each position's own form; manual §7, brief `docs/briefs/2026-11-04-salaries-in-capture.md`): `capture_airlines()` also gives `settings.salary_above_average_pct` (5; null leaves salaries alone). After each airline's fleet pages the userscript reads the game's Employee overview page by `fetch` (`/app/enterprise/staffOverview`, found by its link on the loads page and remembered in the browser): each position's form (hidden `action=salary` and `id`), next week's salary in its `amount` box, and its country average. A position at or above its average is left alone; for each one below it sends that position's own form by `fetch`, as the game's adjust button does (POST to `staffOverview`: `action=salary`, its id, `amount` = the average plus the setting's per cent, rounded to a whole AS$), half a second apart: no page is opened and nothing is clicked. (1.12–1.14 typed into the page's boxes and pressed adjust, but every box is named `amount`, so each position found the first row's box: on 9 Nov the E-jet pilots' salary was set three times and the two positions below were never touched.) It never lowers a salary. The page is read again for the count ("salaries: 2 raised to 5% above the country average", or "all at or above the average"); a form the game refuses is named. A salary page that doesn't read is a line in the panel and skipped: it never stops the capture, never counts as a failed page and never holds back the world scan or the market pages. Nothing about salaries goes to the database.

**The whole statement** (since 10 Nov, version 1.16; spec `docs/specs/2026-11-10-whole-statement.md`): the userscript follows the statement's ">" link to its last page, with the date stop at 26 hours before the capture and a safety stop at 60 pages (a panel line, "statement: stopped at 60 pages, 6,000 lines", and the statement still sent); 1.4 to 1.15 stopped at 15 pages, 1,500 lines, which Ryanair fills in 16 hours. It sends the lines in parts of at most 500 (`STATEMENT_PART_LINES`), a part ending only between two minutes (the database numbers a minute's identical lines and groups its flights one page at a time; a single minute over 500 stays whole), each as page `/app/finance/accounting/2#run=<the run's ISO time>&part=<k>/<n>`, in order, so no one `capture_ingest()` call nears its 30 s limit. The panel says "Ryanair · statement 2,412 lines, 24 pages, 5 parts" (or the first failed part's error); a part refused counts as a failed page. The database judges the run once, on its last part (below). The older userscript (sending names) still works; the one-airline bookmark was retired on 26 Oct; the separate "Salaries: this airline" userscript was retired on 5 Nov.
