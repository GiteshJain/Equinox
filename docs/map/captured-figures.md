# Captured figures

One part of the map, `docs/architecture.md`, in full. The map has the short version; a build that changes this part adds its dated note here, not in the map.

## What it is

The figures worked out from what the capture recorded, in one home, the database (#19, design §8): `cash_forecast()` ("Cash, next 14 days" and when an airline can order, manual §3, with several orders tested together), `airline_cards()` (Performance's scorecards and the group today, Today's orders), `airline_profit()` (profit for each day between captures), `route_profit()` and `aircraft_profit()` (over 1, 3 or 7 days; a route's aircraft hours a flight and an aircraft's flights a day read each leg on its own weekdays, so a 7-day line's route, flown by 7 aircraft once a week each, has the same hours a flight as a plain one and a line aircraft its week's flights over 7 days, held by `database/tests/long_haul_lines_figures_test.sql` since 28 Oct, as is the capture's loads check expecting that weekday's line flights only), `fare_changes()`, `fare_results()` (since 3 Nov, spec `docs/specs/2026-11-03-fare-results.md`: what the fare ladder has done to each direction it repriced, seen by `fare_plan_status()`'s rule over every day kept; its level now by `fare_ladder()`'s rule, worked out from the fare plan so it takes a second or two, not the ladder's 10 to 15; its economy fare, loads and profit a flight from `route_days`, the 7 days before the first seen change against the latest complete days since; the airline's other directions as the baseline; the totals; Performance's Fare results row on each airline's page and a line per airline on the group page, and the weekly report through `export_for_report()`'s kind `fare_results`), `growth_signals()` (Performance's growth signals and the load patterns), `hub_progress()` (hubs first, with contested bases from the latest world scan), `competition()` (since 21 Oct, manual §3 and spec `docs/specs/2026-10-21-competition-analysis.md`: the Competition screen, Today's rival moves and the briefing's openings; from the world scans: the scans used (latest, week before, previous), market share by the airline's markets with the group line, the threats on its pairs (`private.competition_threats()`, which the fare ladder's rival pause reads too), its openings ranked with the default line's variant (since 27 Oct the A350-900 up to `settings.a350_1000_from_km` and the A350-1000 beyond; no E190-E2 note) and hubs first (in November, `settings.trunk_month_from` to `trunk_month_to`, the trunks first, shortest first, hubs first suspended for them, manual §4), and the rival profiles; `private.scan_pair_flights()` reads the scans' schedules on the airline's pairs only; freighters are no one's rivals, by the weekly report's rule, `private.scan_passenger()`; since 23 Oct, spec `docs/specs/2026-10-23-rival-fleets.md` §4, each competitor's and rival profile's `fleet` from its overview page with the change since the week-before scan, `private.scan_fleet()`, and the world-wide `order_flags`, `private.scan_order_flags()`: rivals whose aircraft on order reach `settings.rival_orders_flag_pct` of their fleet with at least `rival_orders_flag_min`, and those with orders and no flights yet, each marked whether it meets the airline; no flag feeds the openings, the contested test or the fare pause; since 23 Oct, spec `docs/specs/2026-10-23-rival-connections.md` §3, each market, opening, rival profile and competitor has `connecting_weekly` with its connecting airports, the rivals' one-stop departures on the airline's pairs from `scan_rival_connections`, beside direct and never added to it: share, threats, the openings' order, the contested test, hubs first, the three biggest competitors and the fare pause read direct only; since 30 Oct, spec `docs/specs/2026-10-30-market-pages-planner.md` §4–5, each market's `game_share`, the game's passenger share over its pairs read in the latest finished market run weighted by each direction's seats in the latest week (`market_weeks`), null with no read, `market_run`, and `contested`: each pair the airline flies where a rival has at least as many departures a week as ours, with each way's economy fare against the cheapest rival's and the game's share this week and last, `private.fig_market_pair()`, which the weekly report's competition section reads through `export_for_report('figures')`), `route_story()` (since 22 Oct, spec `docs/specs/2026-10-21-route-page.md` §2: the route page and the Scheduling chat's "how has this route done since launch, and what changed on it": the route with its launch (since 26 Oct no cabin: the route page works the layout out from the distance with the planning library), now (departures each way, types, the fare ladder's rows), its days and 13 weeks from the daily history by cabin with block hours and profit an aircraft hour, its events (the schedule's saves from `change_log`, the fare plan, rivals between scans by competition analysis's rule) and the rivals on the pair with our share at each scan since launch, since 23 Oct each rival with its connecting departures and airports beside direct, the rivals that only connect, and each scan's (`connecting_only`, `connecting_scans`); since 30 Oct (spec `docs/specs/2026-10-30-market-pages-planner.md` §2) also `days_by_direction`, `rivals.history` (each airline's direct departures at each scan since launch, ours included) and `market` (each direction's latest page read in a finished market run: fares by airline and class, the game's passenger shares this week and last, the pair's seats and price by week; null with no read); `private.fig_route_story()`), `market_service_checks()` (since 30 Oct, spec `docs/specs/2026-10-30-market-pages-planner.md` §3: each pair and direction read in the latest finished market run whose service profile in the game differs from the code its distance calls for, manual §4, or from the planner's codes on the legs that way (the route's rotations), the distance's first; `private.fig_market_service_checks()`; Today's Service in the game, the Systems board's market line, the route page's header),  `fare_ladder()` (since 21 Oct, manual §5: Performance's fare ladder, each route and cabin's level and this Saturday's move with the game actions that make them; since 26 Oct, spec `docs/specs/2026-10-26-fare-ladder-per-direction.md`, **per direction**: a row per route, cabin and direction (out from the hub, back to it; `direction`, `from`, `to`, and `load`, that direction's average load), each direction's level, move, hold after a step down, under-launch jumps and set-back judged on its own flights (and since 26 Oct, spec `docs/specs/2026-10-26-fare-ladder-hold-after-step-up.md`, **the hold after a step up**: a direction whose latest step up is under `fare_ladder_up_hold_weeks` (1) old holds the next Saturday, "held after a step up on 26 Oct", because bookings open 3 days ahead and the week after a change mostly flies at the old price; a jump or a set-back never starts it), a new route, the capacity hold, the rival pause and the 60% floor the whole route's, one game action per direction; its levels from the fare plan's ladder rows, `fare_plan.level_pct` with `fare_plan.direction` (a direction's own latest row, or a both-directions row, `direction` null, if that is later), which `fare_plan_status()` ticks off in their own cabin and, for a row with a direction, on that direction's fares; also since 21 Oct, spec `docs/specs/2026-10-21-frequencies-and-fares.md`: a route that gained a flight holds while its newest rotation is under `fare_ladder_capacity_hold_days` old, the fare index compares each flight of the latest captured day with its direction's fare on 21 Oct 2026, when every route was at its launch level (or its first captured fare, for a route launched later), and since 4 Nov (spec `docs/specs/2026-11-04-fare-default-from-market.md` §3) with the game's default price where the market pages have read that direction (`private.fig_default_prices()`, the latest finished market run by the moment: `default_source` market or inferred, `default_on`, `baseline_fare` the default, `inferred_index` the old reading beside it), and a route more than a point off its level gets set-back changes, `fix`, first in its actions (since 26 Oct a direction, its own change); since 23 Oct, spec `docs/specs/2026-10-23-fare-ladder-under-launch.md`, **under launch**: a cabin at launch averaging under `fare_under_launch_load_pct` (80%) full over `fare_under_launch_days` (14) with at least `fare_under_launch_min_flights` (10) flights **jumps** to the first of `fare_under_launch_levels` (80%, then 60%; Ryanair's own row 75%, then 60%), holds 14 days from the day the captures saw it, and since 26 Oct (spec `docs/specs/2026-10-26-under-launch-by-loads.md`) is then **judged on its loads** like any direction: sold out, it steps up (with the hold after a step up) on through launch to the cap and never jumps again; 90–99% sold out, it holds; under 90% sold out, the next level (since 2 Nov only when also under `fare_under_launch_load_pct` full over the 14 days since the jump, its `under_launch.judged_load` and `judged_flights`, else it holds at its level and is judged again the next Saturday; manual §5), or at the last level it holds there marked `decide`, a cut-or-keep decision for Gitesh (that hold goes on the fare plan once, and the cabin stays marked until it climbs); under launch it only climbs or holds; no revenue test and no revert (`fare_under_launch_decide_load_pct` is no longer read); each row's `ladder_kind` (a jump is move down), `under_launch` and `decide`, and `moves` counts jumps and decisions; the plan card's fare levels and the briefing's `fare_ladder` come from it too), `capture_status()` (the capture board on Systems and Today's capture line; since #21 each airline's latest capture's verdicts too), `route_loads()` (each route's and, since build pull request 6, each flight number's loads over 7 days, the old `loadStats`; for Home's map and Routes), and for Review (build pull request 10; Performance and Systems since 20 Oct) `cash_log()` (each reported day's cash and fixed costs per airline, the old nightly log, with today's figures the cash entry form saves against) and `fare_plan_status()` (each fare change planned against the captured fares, and each route's latest fare). Since build pull request 10 `airline_profit()` also gives each day's profit per aircraft, `route_profit()` the average flight's profit and margin, and the group today each airline's leases and closing together, figures the old Review worked out on its screen. `airline_cards()` also gives the group's average economy load over 7 days (the old Home's figure) and `growth_signals()` whether any profit was captured. Each is a faithful port of the old planner's code, takes an "as of" moment (now by default) and uses only what the capture had recorded by then; staff call them, the scripts get them all through `export_for_report('figures')`. Their builders are `private.fig_*`. The weekly report takes all its money from them since build pull request 4 (no surplus figure, no group totals), and the planner's screens show them. Since the clean-up (build pull request 14) a payment's and a flight's moment use the airport's UTC offset in the world, with its correction (`world_airports.utc_offset_override`), as the schedules do
