# Planning library

One part of the map, `docs/architecture.md`, in full. The map has the short version; a build that changes this part adds its dated note here, not in the map.

## What it is

The planning rules in one home (#19, design §7): local and UTC times, block times, turns, connections (at both ends of a leg, and a hub's city pairs across our airlines), bank windows, maintenance, an aircraft's week and its issues (`tails`, build pull request 7), the game check, daily load report and growth lists (`game`, `loadReport`, `growth`, build pull request 9; Check's, and since 20 Oct Today's, Performance's and Systems'; the Cabins board's `cabins` went with it on 21 Oct; a rotation with no aircraft estimates on the airline's most-flown type, `game.mostFlownType`, its market's default type until then), the service profile a route takes and the plan card's service check (`service`; a leg's code against the one its route's distance calls for, build pull request 10), the plan card's cabin (`card.cabinFor`, 21 Oct, manual §5; by distance since 26 Oct: each route's distance range, `card.cabinRange`, Leisure under `cabin_leisure_max_km`, Leisure Plus to under `cabin_leisure_plus_max_km`, Comfort beyond; the range over `cabin_route_share_pct` of the card's block time decides, otherwise the most seats, Ryanair Standard; `markets.layout` and `routes.cabin_choice` no longer count; `checkCard` flags a card whose cabin is missing or differs), the schedule checks (night bans, the second-daily gap, flight numbers used twice, airport caps, the next free flight numbers), where each aircraft is at a moment, Home's figures (`summary`: key figures, the hero, the airline cards, round trips), the game clocks (`time.clocks`), and the optimiser. TypeScript with no screen code, no clock and no network: plain data in, plain data out. Every number comes from the database through one call, `rules_input(world)` (staff; the new planner asks `rules_input(world, 'flown')`, only the routes with rotations and the airports they, the legs, the bases and the aircraft use), or `export_for_report(secret, 'rules')` for the scripts (always the whole document): settings (an airline's own where it has one), world rules, aircraft types, airports, airlines, bases, aircraft, routes, rotations, every flight leg and the service profiles (since build pull request 10). Its callers since build pull request 4: Claude's schedule optimiser (the command's `optimise`; `scripts/planning/plan.py` is retired), the weekly report (each airline's aircraft, routes, flights, seats and block hours a day, through the command's `aircraft`) and the website's trip search (its connection test, from `web/assets/rules.js`). The planner imports it from source as `@rules` from its screens: Home since pull request 5, for its figures (`summary`), the globe's positions, the game clocks and the attention checks.

**Long haul in 7-day lines** (since 28 Oct, spec `docs/specs/2026-10-27-long-haul-lines.md`, built in #118 to #121; in force, manual §4): `lineOptimiser.optimiseLine` finds the best 7-day rotating line for an airline, a type, its hubs and the candidate routes (one aircraft's 168 hours as a closed walk over the routes from a hub, each flown once each way; feasible on turns, night bans, the same-route gap, the type's reach, maintenance and the spacing; scored by connections within the planning target at the hubs, then all connections, routes covered, block hours, maintenance; a greedy chain through the hubs' bank ladders kept as a beam, then local moves, under a budget of work or a run-time cap, and it says when the search didn't finish; no randomness) and hands back the 7 aircraft cards with their weekday offsets, each leg numbered from the airline's pool; `maintenance.spacing` gives every card's and aircraft's longest stretch without a counting maintenance stop against `settings.maintenance_max_gap_h` (reported in the maintenance figures, flagged by `check-card` and the Tails issues); and `card.checkLine` checks a line of up to 7 cards together (each card, then every flight daily once all 7 exist, the days missing until then, each route both ways daily). The settings `line_min_km`, `maintenance_max_gap_h` and `line_aircraft` came with it (migration `20261028120000`). Claude runs it as a command: `node lib/rules/dist/cli.js` with block, connections, aircraft, check-card (a card or a line file), optimise or optimise-line

## Where

`lib/rules/` (`src/`, its tests in `test/`, hand-made cases in `cases/`, `README.md`); `npm run build` writes the command to `lib/rules/dist/` and `src/browser.ts` to `web/assets/rules.js` (neither committed) **Stations** (since 22 Oct, manual §2; spec `docs/specs/2026-10-22-station-role.md`): an `airline_bases` row with role `station` is an airport flown point-to-point from without making it a base; the library gives it no banks ("station · no banks"), its flight numbers from the airline's one station pool (`station_flight_no_from` to `station_flight_no_to`, 100 to 999), and never counts it as a hub, a focus city or a growth base (`cases/stations.json`).
