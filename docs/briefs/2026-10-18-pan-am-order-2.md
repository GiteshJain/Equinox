**Why B over C:** the 7-day study behind the size guide (manual §3, 18 Oct) found the A320neo earns nearly an A321neo's
profit a flight on 4-star routes (16.6k against 17.1k, about 3% less). That gap (about 3k a day) is less than the lease
saved (0.21M against 0.24M a week, about 4k a day), and B also keeps 0.24M and arrives sooner. C is the choice if the extra
seats matter more on 5-star cities like Minneapolis, which the study didn't cover.

## Scenarios and the risk of waiting
If the A320neo's routes sell out like Pan Am's others, B matches an A321neo's profit after the lease. If they are so strong
that the extra seats would have filled, a later order can take an A321neo onto the busiest of them. Waiting (E) costs about
a day of a new aircraft's profit on each line.
