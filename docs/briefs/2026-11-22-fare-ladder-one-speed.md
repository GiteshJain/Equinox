# The fare ladder: one step and cap for every airline? (22 Nov 2026)

Main chat, on to-do 220 ("ask Gitesh whether he wants a faster fare ladder"). Gitesh, 22 Nov: "i think we should move
pan am and china eastern back to normal speeds as well".

## Decision and recommendation
Should Pan Am and China Eastern go back to the normal fare-ladder step, and to the normal cap too?
The Main chat recommended C (leave as is) unless he had a reason it did not know.

| Option | Step up or down | Cap above launch | Easy to undo |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Normal step, keep their cap | 5 points (was 7) | 28 points, 128% | Yes: two `settings` rows |
| B. Fully normal | 5 points | 20 points, 120% | Yes: four `settings` rows |
| C. Leave as is | 7 points | 28 points, 128% | Nothing to undo |

Growth, cash at the lowest point and opportunity cost were not worked out: the ladder had stepped once, so there was
one week of evidence and no figure worth quoting.

## Background
- **Levels on 22 Nov** (the fare plan's latest row for each direction): Pan Am 167 of 170 directions at 107%, China
  Eastern 240 of 246 at 107%; Kingfisher, Virgin and Malaysia at 105% at most, Ryanair 90%. None above 120%, so B
  strands no fare; the cap starts to matter three steps on (112%, 117%, 120%).
- **The ladder had stepped once,** on 26 Oct. The last fare plan put on was 2 Nov.
- **The one piece of evidence:** the Scheduling note of 4 Nov (to-do 220): after the 26 Oct step-ups of 5 to 7 points,
  loads stayed at 99 to 100% and profit a flight rose 7 to 11% (Ryanair flat). Not re-run on 22 Nov.
- **Why the two had their own step and cap** (22 Oct, `docs/briefs/2026-10-22-pan-am-speed.md`): cash was Pan Am's
  limit and every route sold out; China Eastern was given the same the same day.
- **What would change the recommendation:** a worry about image, or wanting one ladder for all six: then B.

## Outcome
**B, Gitesh, 22 Nov.** No faster ladder for anyone (to-do 220 closed), and Pan Am and China Eastern back to step 5 and
cap 20 points. He gave no reason beyond his words above. Done the same day: the four `settings` rows removed, manual §5,
the planner guide and the rule history.
