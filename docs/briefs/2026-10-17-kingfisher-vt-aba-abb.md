# VT-ABA and VT-ABB, Kingfisher's new A321neos: their routes — 17 Oct 2026

**Decision:** what Kingfisher's two new A321neos fly from Delhi. VT-ABA landed 16 Oct 22:29 UTC; VT-ABB lands 17 Oct
19:29 UTC. The 14 Oct order brief (option A) put frequency first; VT-AAZ took it on 16 Oct (second Kochi and Bhopal,
third Chennai) and said new cities could come with these two. Delhi is wide open: nobody flies any of the cities below
from Delhi except Oman Air (Muscat, 14 a week) and Emirates (Dubai, 7).
**Recommendation and outcome:** A for both. VT-ABA: Bangkok and Doha, about 108k a day, 39 connections, 14.8 hours.
VT-ABB: Guwahati, Muscat and Patna, about 104k a day, 58 connections, 12.7 hours. Five new cities, two of them five-star.

Muscat (1,955) 22k, Guwahati (1,456) 17k, Bhubaneswar (1,278) 16k, Patna (857) 13k, Singapore (4,159) about 36k.
