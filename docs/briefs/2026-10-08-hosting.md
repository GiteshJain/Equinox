## Background
- **Today:** Netlify team "jgitesh88's team" on the Personal plan (Netlify's team record, 8 Oct). Personal costs
  $9 a month for 1,000 credits; a production deploy costs 15 credits, previews cost nothing, bandwidth 20 credits per GB
  (Netlify pricing and credit docs). The free plan's 300 credits are a hard limit: when they run out the site is paused.
  Deploys today are uploads made by Claude; three went out today (45 credits).
- **Cloudflare Pages, free plan:** 500 builds a month, unlimited previews, static files served free and without limit,
  no bandwidth charges; functions get 100,000 requests a day with 10 ms of processing time each (Cloudflare limits and
  Workers pricing pages). Cloudflare's paid Workers plan is $5 a month if we ever need more.
- **What moves:** static files as they are; `netlify.toml`'s redirects and headers become `_redirects` and `_headers`
  files (same format); the game-schedule function becomes a Pages Function at the same address (`/api/game-schedule`).
  Sign-in and all data stay in Supabase; only Supabase's allowed website address changes.
- **The 10 ms limit:** the function waits for the game (waiting doesn't count) and then reads its schedule table. If
  that reading ever exceeds 10 ms, the fix is to send the page to the planner and read it there, or the $5 plan.
- **Domain:** a .com at Cloudflare costs about $10.50 a year at cost. The GitHub Student pack also has free first years
  (Namecheap .me, .TECH, and Name.com's .app/.dev/.live and others), but renewals after year one cost more than a .com.
  The name should avoid the real airlines' names (the site is a fan project).
- **Why a domain matters:** with our own address, any future change of host is invisible to readers. (Corrected
  8 Oct: no links to the site have been shared yet, so changing the address now costs nothing.)

## Scenarios and the risk of waiting
- **Staying on Netlify:** fine for daily work, but the rebuild phases (decisions #13 to #20) will mean many deploys;
  each costs credits, and the monthly credits check stays a chore.
- **Moving:** a preview on Cloudflare is tested before anything switches; the Netlify site keeps working throughout.
  If something fails, the switch simply doesn't happen.
- **Waiting:** every link shared on the Netlify address is one more to redirect later.

## What would change the recommendation
- If the game-schedule function needs more than the free processing time and reading it in the planner isn't wanted:
  B still works with the $5 Workers plan, still cheaper than Netlify.
- If you'd rather not create a Cloudflare account or hold a card there for the domain: A or C.
