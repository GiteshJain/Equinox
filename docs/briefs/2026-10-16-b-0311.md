# B-0311, China Eastern's new A321neo: its routes — 16 Oct 2026

**Decision:** what B-0311 flies from Shanghai PVG. It landed 16 Oct 01:34 UTC; ordered for new cities (14 Oct China
Eastern brief, option A: Singapore, Saigon, Don Mueang, Manila, Hanoi, Sanya, Urumqi and others). Its three sister
aircraft land tonight and Saturday, so this is the first of three.
**Recommendation:** A, Singapore and Manila. Singapore is the only five-star city left in reach (Singapore Airlines
flies it twice a day) and the longest route an A321neo can do from Shanghai; Manila is four-star with no rival. About
120k a day, 69 connections, 15.1 hours a day, maintenance 170%. Saigon and Don Mueang go to B-0312 tomorrow.

## Background
- **China Eastern** (briefing, 16 Oct 03:36 UTC): profit 0.63M a day over 7 days, load 99%, cash 3.24M with 2.70M at
  the lowest point; the 13 refits wait for Monday.
- **Hubs first:** Shanghai has 301 departures a week of 750; all three new aircraft are Shanghai's.
- **Competition** (6 Oct scan): Singapore Airlines 14 a week Shanghai–Singapore; nobody on the other cities.
- **Kolkata, a warning:** the first captured day of Shanghai–Kolkata (B-0170, launched 13 Oct) carried 4% of economy
  out and 3% back, losing about 4.7k a flight, while every other route launched that day sold out. One day only; it is
  raised as its own item, and it argues against India–China flying (the Delhi XLR idea in the 14 Oct brief).

## Asked the same night: Singapore plus a few smaller routes?
Two short round trips beside Singapore don't fit: every pair tried (Hefei with Wuhan, Changsha, Fuzhou, Qingdao, Xiamen,
Zhengzhou, Nanjing or Nanchang; Wuhan with Fuzhou; Qingdao with Fuzhou) left no gap long enough for maintenance. One
fits: Singapore + Wuhan (second daily, ~109k a day, 67 links, 12.5 h) or + Changsha (~109k, 12.9 h), both below
Singapore + Manila because Manila is longer.
