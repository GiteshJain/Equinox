# N018AW, Pan Am's new A321XLR: its route — 17 Oct 2026

**Decision:** what N018AW flies from JFK. It lands 17 Oct 19:25 UTC; ordered for frequency on the sold-out Atlantic routes
(14 Oct Pan Am brief, option C). An XLR crossing the Atlantic flies one round trip a day, with room at most for one short
hop.
**Recommendation:** first A (a second Dublin daily with a third Dulles, ~118k a day, 44 links), then D (Lisbon with
Dulles, ~122k) when Gitesh asked whether Barcelona could take a short hop (it can't). **Chosen: C,** Barcelona: about
