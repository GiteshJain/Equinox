## Scenarios and the risk of waiting
- **C goes well:** Barcelona's five-star routes sell like Amsterdam–Rome or Lisbon (20–26k a flight), and Amsterdam's
  second dailies hold their first flights' figures. **Badly:** Barcelona's routes sell like Paris–Munich (14k a flight).
  That's still above the lease, and the aircraft can move to Paris or Amsterdam at any time.
- **A** is the safest money but adds few new five-star markets. **B** commits everything to an untested base.
- **Waiting:** each day an A321neo isn't flying gives up about 80k of flight profit, and rivals could take Barcelona's
  open five-star routes.

## What would change the recommendation
- Preferring proven routes only: A.
- Wanting Barcelona to open with more aircraft from day one: B.
- A rival opening Barcelona–Zurich, Munich or Dublin in the next scan: B, to secure them.
