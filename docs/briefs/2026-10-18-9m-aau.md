# 9M-AAU, Malaysia's new A321neo: its routes — 18 Oct 2026

**Decision:** what 9M-AAU flies from Kuala Lumpur. It lands 19 Oct 03:34 UTC. It was ordered for frequency on sold-out
routes (17 Oct Malaysia order brief), but until 31 Oct every new flight must open a new route (manual §4), and hubs first
keeps it at KUL (308 of 750 departures a week).
**Recommendation:** A, Colombo + Dhaka: two new 4-star capitals nobody flies from KUL, 13.8 hours a day and the most profit
(~0.56M a week after the lease), both in one market so one cabin fits.

Profit is estimated from Malaysia's own A321neo routes (route profit, 7 days to 18 Oct, the airline briefing): Kolkata,
Chennai, Hong Kong, Guangzhou and Bengaluru (2,500–2,900 km) 28–31k a flight; Ho Chi Minh City and Jakarta (1,000–1,150 km)
about 18k; Delhi (3,850 km) 44.9k. So Colombo and Manila about 28k, Dhaka about 29k, Bali and Hanoi about 25k, Changsha
about 31k, Taipei about 33k, Don Mueang about 17k. The A321neo's lease is about 0.24M a week (manual §9).

## Background
- **Malaysia** (briefing, 18 Oct 19:57 UTC): profit 0.84M a day over 7 days, load 96%, cash 4.23M (its lowest point is
  now). Trivandrum (24%) and Kochi (74%) are the weak routes; the rest sell out or nearly.
- **Strategy** (manual §2): KUL is to become a world transfer hub, with the Asian feeder network first. Colombo and Dhaka
  bring South Asian passengers onto Malaysia's China, Japan and ASEAN flights; Manila and Bali (option B) bring
  ASEAN passengers onto its India flights.
- **Ownership** (manual §2): pairs with Malaysia belong to Malaysia.

## Scenarios and the risk of waiting
If both sell like Kolkata and Chennai, A matches Malaysia's middle A321neo. Waiting leaves the aircraft idle from 03:34 UTC.

## What would change the recommendation
- Preferring the big ASEAN markets first: B (Bali and Manila), at about 0.04M a week less.
