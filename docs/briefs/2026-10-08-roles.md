## Background (live checks, 8 Oct)
- **Who exists today:**
  - 1 sign-in account (yours, email and password), and 1 email on the staff list (`planner_users`).
  - 1 capture key (hashed) and 1 investor password (hashed).
  - 1 ingest secret, stored in plain text in the `ingest_secret` table.
- **How each kind of user gets in today:**
  - **Public:** the website reads everything through `site_public()` and `site_newsletter()`. These are public by
    design.
  - **Staff (you):** sign in to the planner. Every table's row rule checks `is_planner_user()`, meaning your email is on
    the staff list.
  - **Investors:** one shared password opens the investor page and reports, through `site_investor()` and
    `site_report()`.
  - **The capture:** the userscript sends a capture key that `capture_ingest()` checks against a hash. It's revocable.
  - **Claude's scripts:** send the ingest secret to 8 functions. Five export or receive data
    (`export_capture`, `export_for_report`, `export_planner_data`, `export_repo_bootstrap`, `ingest_snapshot`). The
    other three write to the website and the planner's research (`site_admin`, `planner_data_set`, `save_script`).
- **Gaps (Supabase's security advisor and the documentation work, 8 Oct):**
  1. **Accounts can be created by anyone.** The planner has a sign-up form. A new account isn't staff, but once its
     email is confirmed it is "signed in", which is enough to:
     - read flight profit through the two views (`game_flight_pnl`, `game_ops_recent` run as their owner and skip the
       staff check);
     - call two internal capture steps directly (`apply_loads_from_capture` rewrites the planner's monthly loads from
       the capture; `parse_capture` re-parses a stored page).

     Only your account exists today, so this hasn't happened.
  2. **One plain-text secret opens 8 functions,** including rewriting the website (`site_admin`). It's never in the
     repository or the backups, but it sits in plain text in the database and in a file in Claude's workspace.
  3. **13 functions can be called without signing in.** The advisor lists them all, but on inspection 2 are public by
     design, 1 checks the capture key, 2 check the investor password and 8 check the ingest secret.
  4. **Minor:** `as_num()` has no fixed search path. Leaked-password protection is off, and it needs the paid plan.
- **What B would build:**
  - **A `roles` table** (who, which role: owner, staff, investor, automation) and one check, `has_role()`, used by every
    row rule and function in place of the staff email list. Adding a person is one row. A group column can be added at
    #14 if the planner-only version for other players goes ahead, so access wouldn't need redoing.
  - **Accounts by invitation only:** you turn off "Allow new users to sign up" in Supabase, and staff are invited from
    the dashboard. The planner's sign-up form goes.
  - **The gaps closed:**
    - the two views follow the staff rule;
    - the two internal capture steps can only be called by the capture itself;
    - `as_num()` gets a fixed search path.
  - **Claude's own account** with the automation role, replacing the ingest secret:
    - the 8 functions check the role instead of a secret, and stop answering anyone not signed in;
    - the secret and its table are deleted;
    - the account's password never goes in the repository or chat, and it's set up through a to-do, like the backups
      key.

    Unattended jobs (#22) will use the same account, holding its password as a GitHub secret.
  - **Unchanged:** the capture key (already hashed, per-device and revocable); the investor password (one read-only
    password; individual investor sign-ins are option C); the public website.
  - **Proof:** the database rehearsal (#8) tries the changes on last night's copy. A new check signs in as each role
    (public, an account that isn't staff, staff, automation) against the copy and confirms each can do exactly what it
    should and nothing more.
- **Cost facts (Supabase docs):**
  - Leaked-password protection is available on the Pro plan and above.
  - Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app has no extra charge. Only phone-based two-step sign-in is a paid add-on
    ($75 a month).

## Scenarios and the risk of waiting
- **If nothing changes:** the most likely problem is not an attacker but a stray sign-up, for example a curious player
  from the group's Discord who finds the planner's sign-up form. They could read every route's profit, which is
  competitive information in a game where others are competing for the same markets. The worst case is a leak of the
  ingest secret: someone could rewrite the website and the planner's research. Recovery would be from last night's
  backup.
- **The rebuild (#13 onwards):** new tables need access rules. With B, each new rule is one `has_role()` check that the
  rehearsal tests. Without it, every table repeats the email-list check, and #14's multi-player question reopens all of
  them.
- **Waiting:** the gaps exist now, but only your account exists, so the exposure is small until someone signs up or the
  secret leaks.

## What would change the recommendation
- **If you plan to give the investor page to more than a handful of people,** or want to know who opened which report:
  C's individual investor sign-ins.
- **If you want the strongest protection on your own account,** which can change everything: add C's two-step sign-in.
  It's free, and about an hour of planner work.
- **If #17 moves Supabase to the paid plan anyway:** turn on leaked-password protection then.
