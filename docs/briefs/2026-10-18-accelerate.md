# Adding aircraft every day — 18 Oct 2026

**Recommendation and outcome:** C. Orders every evening the forecast allows, a route list per airline agreed each
Saturday, every aircraft filed within a day of arrival, and more profit per aircraft (fare tests on raise-fares routes,
more flying hours per aircraft, the A320neo on 3-star routes).

## The question
Gitesh, 18 Oct: how do we get to adding several aircraft every day?

## Options

| Option | What changes | Rough pace | Gitesh's time |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. As now | Orders in batches; routes decided one aircraft at a time | about 1 a day | an hour or two per batch |
| B. A daily rhythm | Orders every evening the forecast allows; a route list per airline each Saturday; filed within a day | about 2 a day | 10–15 min a day, about an hour on Saturdays |
| **C. B plus more profit per aircraft** | **B, plus fare tests on raise-fares routes, more block hours per aircraft, A320neo on 3-star routes** | **about 2.5–3 a day within weeks** | **as B** |

Paces are estimates from the week to 17 Oct, not forecasts.

## Background (17–18 Oct)
- **Cash:** the group made about 4.9M a day after leases and salaries over the 7 days to 17 Oct, rising as the week's
  aircraft fly (flight profit 10.0M on 17 Oct against 7.7M on 13 Oct). A new A321neo takes a security deposit of
  about 2.4M (an XLR about 2.9M): roughly two aircraft a day.
- **Delivery lines** don't bind: per airline, an A320-family aircraft every 21 hours and an E195 every 15 (manual §3).
- **The limits were the process:** orders went in as weekly batches, each arrival waited for its own route discussion,
  and aircraft waited days to be filed (10 on 17–18 Oct). A parked aircraft forgoes about 0.09M of flight profit a day
  and its lease starts 7 days after arrival regardless.
- **Profit per aircraft:** block hours a day per aircraft on 17 Oct: Malaysia 13.3, Pan Am 12.7, Kingfisher 12.5,
  China Eastern 11.9, Virgin 11.2, Ryanair 11.0. Each extra hour is about 8% more flying from aircraft already paid for.

**Trade-off:** routes are decided weekly in a batch instead of one aircraft at a time (Gitesh's rule of 18 Oct
morning). Every card is still seen before filing; a card that leaves the list comes back as options first.

## Outcome
**18 Oct: C.** Manual §3 (orders every evening), §4 (route lists, filing within a day, flying hours) and §7 (the
Saturday review); the old rule moved to Retired rules. The first route lists come at the Saturday review on 24 Oct; the
evening order check starts with the 18 Oct capture.
