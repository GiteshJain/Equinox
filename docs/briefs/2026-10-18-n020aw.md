# N020AW, Pan Am's first E195: its routes — 18 Oct 2026

**Decision:** what N020AW flies from JFK. It lands 18 Oct 21:31 UTC, Pan Am's first E195 (ordered 17 Oct). Until
31 Oct every new flight must open a new route (manual §4), and hubs first keeps it at JFK (196 of 750 departures a week).
**Recommendation:** C, Burlington + Charleston + Savannah + Syracuse: four new cities, 83 links at JFK and the most profit
(~0.29M a week after the lease), all four routes a real airline flies from JFK.

Three ~1,000 km rotations plus a short hop doesn't fit: no ground gap long enough for maintenance. Two ~1,000 km rotations
and two short hops do. Four short New England hops (Syracuse, Burlington, Portland, Rochester) fit but give only 7.3 hours
a day.

Profit is an estimate: Pan Am has no E195 yet. The group's E195s (7 days to 18 Oct, route_days) earn 9–13k a flight at
1,000–1,200 km on 3-star routes (Krakow, Split, Cam Ranh, Kota Kinabalu) and 4–9k on hops under 500 km. Pan Am's own
A321neo domestic flights earn 13.7k (Washington National, 340 km) to 18.3k (Nashville, 1,230 km); the E195 earns about
0.6 of an A321neo a flight. So ~10k a flight to Charleston and Savannah, ~7k to Burlington and Syracuse, ~6k to
Providence and Albany. The E195's lease is about 0.18M a week (manual §9).

## Background
- **Pan Am** (briefing, 18 Oct 18:58 UTC): profit 0.96M a day over 7 days, load 99%, cash 4.12M, lowest point 3.34M
  on 19 Oct. Every route sells out but Toronto (fix, 76%).
- **Competition** (world scan 4, 17 Oct): nobody flies JFK to Burlington, Syracuse, Charleston, Savannah, Louisville,
  Providence or Albany. Of the short candidates only Boston has rivals (Continental 24 a week, United 14).
- **Size by city** (manual §3): the E195 is for 2-star cities and short thin routes. These are 3-star secondary cities;
  the E195 is the aircraft we have, and the routes can move to an A320neo later if they outgrow it.

## Scenarios and the risk of waiting
If the short hops sell out like every other Pan Am route, C earns most; if they disappoint, Burlington or Syracuse can
be swapped for a third ~1,000 km city (option A's shape) at no cost. Waiting leaves the aircraft idle from 21:31 UTC.

- Wanting fewer cycles and longer flights: A.
