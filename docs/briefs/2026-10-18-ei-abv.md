# EI-ABV, Ryanair's new E195 at Paris: its routes — 18 Oct 2026

**Decision:** what EI-ABV flies from Paris CDG. It lands 18 Oct 21:33 UTC (ordered 17 Oct). Until 31 Oct every new flight
must open a new route (manual §4); Ryanair is exempt from hubs first.
**Recommendation:** A, Bilbao + Thessaloniki + Malta: three new cities nobody flies from Paris, 88 links, 13 hours a day
and the most profit (~0.28M a week after the lease).

Two long rotations alone (Malta and Thessaloniki, or Catania and Tallinn) give only 10 hours and 58 links; a short third
hop adds 30 links. Four cities don't fit (maintenance).

**Why 3-star cities:** by the size guide (manual §3), the 4-star cities still open from Paris (Seville, Stockholm,
Helsinki, Venice, Hamburg, Berlin) are A321neo routes, and Ryanair's EI-ABU (A321neo, CDG) lands on 20 Oct.

Profit is estimated from Ryanair's own E195s at CDG (route_days, 7 days to 18 Oct, all sold out): Sofia (1,754 km, 3-star)
12.2k a flight, Bari (1,414) 11.5k, Split (1,216) 11.1k, Krakow (1,246) 8.6k; under 800 km 4.6–8.8k. So Malta and
Thessaloniki about 12.5k, Palermo, Dubrovnik, Catania and Vilnius 11–12k, Zagreb, Cagliari and Gothenburg about 10k,
Bilbao about 8k, Montpellier about 7k. The E195's lease is about 0.18M a week (manual §9).

## Background
- **Ryanair** (briefing, 18 Oct 19:14 UTC): profit 1.08M a day over 7 days, load 100%, cash 2.87M (its lowest point is
  now). Every route sells out.
- **Competition** (world scan 4, 17 Oct): nobody flies Paris to Bilbao, Thessaloniki, Malta, Palermo, Dubrovnik, Zagreb,
  Catania, Vilnius, Montpellier, Cagliari or Gothenburg. Air France flies Faro, Bordeaux, Bologna, Cork and Bucharest once
  a day; airBaltic Riga 4 a day.
- **Ownership** (manual §2): UK cities stay with Virgin, so Leeds, Liverpool, Newcastle, Belfast and Glasgow are out.

## Scenarios and the risk of waiting
A's Malta and Thessaloniki are the longest 3-star routes in reach and should earn most; if Bilbao's short hop earns
little, it can be swapped for another city at no cost. Waiting leaves the aircraft idle from 21:33 UTC.

## What would change the recommendation
- Wanting more maintenance slack, or no 4 am arrival: B.
- Wanting the Baltic opened: C (Vilnius).

## Outcome
**18 Oct: A,** Bilbao, Thessaloniki and Malta, as above. Plan card in the planner (cabin E195-STD); logged as a
feeder move.
