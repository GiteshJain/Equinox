# Kingfisher's Amsterdam XLR to a second Heathrow daily? — 15 Oct 2026

**Decision:** what VT-AAE, Kingfisher's A321XLR on Delhi–Amsterdam, flies from now on. Raised as its own item in the
14 Oct Kingfisher order brief; the route is on Needs attention ("fix or reduce", economy averaging 49%).
**Recommendation:** A, move it to a second Delhi–Heathrow daily, leaving Delhi at 11:00 and Heathrow at 15:12.
Heathrow sells out in both cabins every day at 56–60k a flight; Amsterdam fills about half its economy seats at 30–33k.
A second London daily should make about 35k a day more, with no money spent, and it can be switched back.

Profit is from game_flights, 5–14 Oct: VT-AAE makes about 63k a day on Amsterdam (two flights at 30k and 33k) against a
lease of about 41k. VT-AAC makes about 117k a day on Heathrow (56k and 60k). A second Heathrow daily at about 85%
economy would make about 50k a flight, roughly 100k a day. That's an estimate: how much London demand is left over
isn't visible, because the one daily is full.

## Background
- **Loads** (game_flights, 5–14 Oct): Delhi–Heathrow 100% economy and 100% business on every flight, both ways.
  Delhi–Paris 85–91% economy, 50–52k a flight. Delhi–Amsterdam 42–58% economy (last six nights: 75, 42, 42, 46, 46,
  58), business 98–100%, 30–33k a flight. All three fly the 141-seat XLR cabin (125 economy, 16 business).
- **Competition** (6 Oct world scan): nobody else flies Delhi–London, Delhi–Amsterdam, Delhi–Paris, Frankfurt, Munich,
  Zurich or Rome. Turkish flies Delhi–Istanbul 14 a week.
- **Timing** (the planning library's optimiser on the 15 Oct tables): the best second London daily leaves Delhi at
  11:00 (Heathrow 13:56) and Heathrow at 15:12 (Delhi 05:08 next morning), 6½ hours from the first daily's 17:30
  departure. 18 connections at Delhi; maintenance well clear (the aircraft flies 16.9 hours a day).
- **Ownership:** UK–India is Kingfisher's under one airline per city pair.
- **Amsterdam after:** no group airline would fly Delhi–Amsterdam. Ryanair's Amsterdam network gets no Delhi feed from
  Kingfisher, which is small today (half-empty economy).

## Scenarios and the risk of waiting
- **A goes well:** the second daily fills like the first, and VT-AAE makes about 115k a day, 50k more than now.
  **Badly:** it fills to 60%, about Amsterdam's money, and the first daily dips a little. Switching back is a retime.
- **B** could match Paris, or repeat Amsterdam.
- **Waiting** costs about 35k a day if A works as expected.

## What would change the recommendation
- Wanting another new city on the map rather than more London: B, Frankfurt first.
- A rival opening Delhi–London in the next scan: A all the more.
